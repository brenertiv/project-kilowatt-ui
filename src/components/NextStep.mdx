# NextStep

`NextStep` is a page's note bar: one bar under the page header that tells the person where things stand, in a few words that name the problem. The bar is the page's width and at least 48px tall, on `background.paper` with a `border.level0` edge and the theme radius. A mark leads it (`icon`, the app's assistant mark), the note follows in `subtitle2` and `secondary.main`, and a label ends it: **Explain**, with the `chat` icon, which hands the whole note to the app's assistant. The mark, the note and the label are one MUI `ButtonBase` (`@mui/material`, Material UI v9) with the hover tint, and a Dismiss `IconButton` sits beside it. A page with nothing to do says so in the same bar (“Nothing to do here: …”), with no label, and that bar is not a button. It's Kilowatt's own component, built on MUI's `ButtonBase`, `IconButton`, `Tooltip` and `Typography`, whose props are MUI's: see the [ButtonBase API](https://mui.com/material-ui/api/button-base/) and [IconButton API](https://mui.com/material-ui/api/icon-button/). `CardNextStep`, from the same file, is a step on a clickable Overview card: a plain caption line under the card's figure, because the whole card is the control.

## When to use

- Use `NextStep` under a page header, as the one bar between the title and the page's content, when the page has a note for the person: a problem, or that there is nothing to do.
- It is the one thing that sits under a page title. The rules (Rules → Content and layout) keep a line of description out from under a title; this bar is not a description of the page but a note about its state. Don't let it carry what the page is for; that goes in the title.
- The bar doesn't offer the page's own step. Put the step itself (a button that names the contacts, an upload) in the page's content or in `PageHeader`'s `actions`.
- Use `CardNextStep` for a step on an Overview card that opens its page: a caption under the figure, so a count isn't left on its own. The card's `CardActionArea` is the one control; opening the card leads to where the step is taken.
- For a result that appears on its own (a save finished, a request failed), use `Snackbar` or `Alert`; this bar is the page's standing state, not an event.

## Usage

Provide what Explain does once, round the app. The handler gets the whole `state`, and opens the app's assistant on it:

```tsx
import { NextStep, NextStepExplain } from 'src/components/NextStep';

<NextStepExplain value={(state) => openAssistant(`Explain this: ${state}`)}>
  {app}
</NextStepExplain>
```

Then, on a page:

```tsx
<PageHeader title="Properties" />
<NextStep
  icon={assistantMark}
  state="3 of 12 properties have no named contact. Reports for them go to the account owner."
  action={{ label: 'Name the Contacts', onClick: openContacts }}
/>
```

The bar shows the note in a few words: `summary`, or `shortNote(state)`, which keeps the first sentence and drops what follows a comma, a semicolon, a bracket or a colon (a label of one or two words before a colon stays). Here it reads “3 of 12 properties have no named contact”, and Explain hands on both sentences. `action` is the step the page has; the bar never draws or calls it, and reads it only to tell a note with something to do, which has Explain, from one without. `icon` is a slot: Kilowatt holds no assistant mark, so the app passes its own, 24px and decorative.

A page with nothing to do:

```tsx
<NextStep icon={assistantMark} state="Nothing to do here: every property has a named contact." />
```

A bar with its own label and action, in place of Explain:

```tsx
<NextStep icon={assistantMark} state="Six things need a decision this week." ask={{ label: 'Help Me Prioritize', onClick: openAssistant }} />
```

On a clickable Overview card, under the figure:

```tsx
import { CardNextStep } from 'src/components/NextStep';

<CardActionArea onClick={openProperties}>
  <CardHeader title={<TruncatedText>Properties</TruncatedText>} />
  <CardContent>
    <PlainFigure value="12" />
    <CardNextStep>Name the 3 missing contacts</CardNextStep>
  </CardContent>
</CardActionArea>
```

## States

- With an action, under a `NextStepExplain` provider: the whole bar is a button, the note then Explain with the `chat` icon. Pressing it calls the provider's handler with the whole `state`.
- With `ask`: the bar is a button with `ask.label` in place of Explain, and pressing it calls `ask.onClick`.
- Nothing to do: no `action` and no `ask`. The bar is the note alone, not a button, and the state says so: “Nothing to do here: …”.
- No provider: a note with an `action` but no `NextStepExplain` provider has no Explain and is not a button.
- Hover and focus: the bar takes `action.hover` under the pointer, and the button takes `action.focus` with the focus ring on keyboard focus.
- Dismissed: Dismiss removes the bar until the page is opened again. Nothing is stored, so a note that still holds comes back.

## Do and don't

- Do write the state as one sentence that names the problem, with its numbers: “3 of 12 properties have no named contact.”
- Don't name the page or use a status word alone (“Properties: incomplete”); say what is wrong.
- Do keep what the bar shows to a few words, through `summary` where `shortNote` cuts badly.
- Don't fit the whole situation into the bar; Explain opens it in the assistant.
- Do provide `NextStepExplain` once, round the app, so every page's note explains the same way.
- Don't open a dialog from Explain; it hands the note to the app's assistant.
- Do put the page's own step in the page's content or in `PageHeader`'s `actions`.
- Don't add a second button to the bar; it has its one label and Dismiss.
- Do pass the app's assistant mark as `icon`, the same mark on every page.
- Don't use the mark to carry a status or a severity; the note's words do that.
- Do use `CardNextStep` on a card whose whole surface is a `CardActionArea`.
- Don't put a `NextStep` or any button inside a clickable card; a button can hold no other control, so the step is plain text there.
- Do let the note and the label take `secondary.main` and the bar its paper surface, as the component sets them.
- Don't recolor or fill the bar with `sx` to make it louder; its place under the title is what marks it.

## MUI departures

- `focus-ring`: keyboard focus on the bar's button and on Dismiss is a 2px ring in `palette.control.focus`, the brand blue; the bar's button also takes `action.focus` as its fill.
- `icon-button-radius`: Dismiss takes the theme radius (8px) instead of MUI's circle.
- `icons`: the `chat` and `close` icons are Material Symbols through `Icon`, not `@mui/icons-material`.
- `medium-size`: Dismiss stays at `IconButton`'s medium size, the default.

The label is set in the `button` type style, so it is shown as written, in Title Case. Theme-wide departures (the palette, the type scale, the corner radius) apply too; see Foundations → MUI departures.

## Accessibility

- The bar is a `status` region named “Next step”, so a screen reader can find it and hears a change to it without it taking focus.
- With Explain or `ask`, the mark, the note and the label are one native `button` (MUI's `ButtonBase`), named by the note and the label together (“3 of 12 properties have no named contact Explain”), with keyboard activation and the focus ring.
- Dismiss is an `IconButton` named “Dismiss”, with a tooltip saying the same, beside the bar's button, not inside it.
- The `chat` and `close` icons are MUI `Icon`s, hidden from assistive technology. The `icon` slot is decorative: pass a mark that is hidden from assistive technology too.
- Explain hands on the whole `state`, so what the bar's few words leave out is not lost to anyone.
- `CardNextStep` is plain text inside the card's `CardActionArea`, so it becomes part of the card's accessible name; keep it short.
