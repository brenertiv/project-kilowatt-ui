# RadioGroup

A radio group lets people choose exactly one of a few options, all shown at once. Kilowatt uses MUI's `RadioGroup` and `Radio` from `@mui/material` (Material UI v9) with no wrapper, each radio labelled with `FormControlLabel`. Props are MUI's: see the [RadioGroup API](https://mui.com/material-ui/api/radio-group/) and [Radio API](https://mui.com/material-ui/api/radio/).

## When to use

- Use a radio group for one choice from two to five options, when seeing them all helps people choose.
- For more options, or when space is short, use `Select`.
- For a view switch in a toolbar (chart or table, day or week), use `ToggleButtonGroup`.
- For one option that's on or off, use `Checkbox` or `Switch`, not a pair of radios.

## Usage

```tsx
import FormControl from '@mui/material/FormControl';
import FormControlLabel from '@mui/material/FormControlLabel';
import FormLabel from '@mui/material/FormLabel';
import Radio from '@mui/material/Radio';
import RadioGroup from '@mui/material/RadioGroup';

<FormControl>
  <FormLabel id="interval-label">Reading Interval</FormLabel>
  <RadioGroup
    aria-labelledby="interval-label"
    name="interval"
    value={interval}
    onChange={(event) => setInterval(event.target.value)}
  >
    <FormControlLabel value="15" control={<Radio />} label="15 Minutes" />
    <FormControlLabel value="60" control={<Radio />} label="Hourly" />
  </RadioGroup>
</FormControl>;
```

## Variants

Stacked by default; `row` lays two or three short options on one line. `size` on `Radio` is `small` or `medium` (the default). `color` takes a palette intent; leave it at `primary`.

## States

- `error` on the `FormControl`, with `FormHelperText`: a required choice is missing. Say what to pick.
- `disabled` on a `FormControlLabel` (or the whole `FormControl`): the option can't be chosen here. Say why nearby.

## Do and don't

- Do give the group a `FormLabel` and tie it to the `RadioGroup` with `aria-labelledby`.
- Don't leave a group unnamed; each radio's label alone doesn't say what's being chosen.
- Do preselect the most common or safest option when there is one.
- Don't preselect an option that commits people to something costly; leave the group empty and mark it `required`.
- Do order options logically: smallest to largest, or most to least common.
- Don't use a radio group for more than about five options; use `Select`.

## MUI departures

No departure changes `RadioGroup` or `Radio` on their own.

Theme-wide departures (CSS variables, surface and border levels, the navy primary for the checked radio and the other intent colors) apply too; see Foundations → MUI departures.

## Accessibility

- MUI renders native radio inputs sharing one `name`, so arrow keys move the choice within the group and Tab moves past it.
- `aria-labelledby` on `RadioGroup` names the group from its `FormLabel`.
- `FormControlLabel` makes each label part of its radio's name and click target.

## Related

- `Select` for longer lists.
- `ToggleButtonGroup` for view switches.
- `Checkbox` for options people can combine.
