# Site map

A site map shows where a portfolio's buildings are, and can carry one reading per site. `SiteMap` is Kilowatt's reference implementation: MapLibre GL (`maplibre-gl`) draws CARTO's basemap from `basemapStyle`, themed for the active color scheme, and deck.gl (`@deck.gl/layers`) draws each site as a dot in a color from `mapColors` (Rules → Maps). Enertiv's app rebuilds its maps from this one rather than importing it.

<Canvas of={SiteMapStories.Sites} />

## When to use

- Use a site map when where the sites are is the point: which buildings are near each other, which region a problem is in.
- To compare readings across sites, use a column chart or a table; a dot's color can't show a value precisely. Use the map beside them, not in place of them.
- For a measure over time at one site, use a line chart.
- Past six kinds of site, group them or show a table, as with chart series.

## Usage

```tsx
import Card from '@mui/material/Card';
import CardHeader from '@mui/material/CardHeader';
import { SiteMap } from 'src/maps/SiteMap';

const sites = buildings.map(({ id, name, latitude, longitude }) => ({ id, name, latitude, longitude }));

<Card variant="outlined">
  <CardHeader title="Buildings" />
  <SiteMap sites={sites} summary="Seven buildings in Manhattan, from the Financial District to Midtown." />
</Card>
```

An app that rebuilds the map hosts `src/maps/fonts` itself and passes its glyphs URL to `basemapStyle`. With nested routes (`/company/scope/page`), resolve it from the site root, `mapGlyphsUrl(new URL('/', document.baseURI).href)`, since the default resolves from the page's own URL.

Give each site a `value` and the map a `unit` to show a reading in each site's tooltip. Leave out sites without coordinates; the map opens fitted to the sites it's given. Keep `sites` stable (a constant or `useMemo`), since the layer is rebuilt when it changes.

## Variants

- **Sites.** Every site in the first series color. Give a site a `slot` when it stands for a product meaning (a building type), so it keeps one color on every map and chart.
- **Highlight.** The site that matters in a series color, and the rest `neutral`: smaller, gray and drawn underneath, so the highlighted site reads first by size as well as color.
- **Status.** Each site takes an intent through `status` (`success`, `warning`, `error`, `info`) in place of `slot`, with a `detail` line in its tooltip and the list saying what the color stands for. A site with no data for what the map shows is `hollow`, a ring in its color, rather than left off. Don't mix `status` and `slot` on one map.
- **Sized.** `sized` scales each dot by its `value` on a square-root scale, from 12px to 32px across, so a dot's area follows its value; a site without a value takes the smallest size. The exact value stays in the tooltip.

Pass `onSelect` to make each dot open its site (a property page, say): the dot shows the pointer, and the site is passed to the callback. Keep it stable with `useCallback`.

## Do and don't

- Do put the map in an outlined `Card` (`variant="outlined"`) under a title-only `CardHeader`, straight in the card: the map runs edge to edge below the header, and the card clips it to its corners.
- Don't wrap the map in `CardContent` or give it a border of its own; the card is its frame.
- Do write `summary` as one sentence that says where the sites are and what stands out.
- Don't leave the summary generic ("Map"); it's what screen readers say for the whole map.
- Do mark context sites `neutral` when one site is the point.
- Do give every status site a `detail` that says in words what its color means; color is never the only signal.
- Don't set a color in map code or pass a CARTOColors scheme; take colors from `mapColors`.
- Do keep CARTO's attribution; its terms and OpenStreetMap's license require it.
- Don't turn off cooperative gestures; a map that zooms on every scroll traps the page.

## MUI departures

- `chart-palette`: sites take `chart.series` by `slot`, and neutral sites take `chart.neutral`.
- `map-palette`: the basemap's land, water, road casings and boundaries take `map.land`, `map.water`, `map.road` and `map.boundary`.

Theme-wide departures (the surface levels the card and basemap use, the theme radius) apply too; see Foundations → MUI departures.

## CARTO departures

How this map differs from stock CARTO and MapLibre, from `src/maps/departures.ts`; Foundations → CARTO departures has the reasons.

- `basemap-surfaces`: the land is `map.land`, a step past the page; land use `background.level1`, buildings `border.level2`, water `map.water`.
- `basemap-roads`: roads are filled with `background.level2` and edged in `map.road`.
- `basemap-boundaries`: boundaries are drawn in `map.boundary`, a pixel wide at the least.
- `basemap-low-zoom`: state lines, motorways and trunk roads and land cover draw from zoom 3, and rivers a pixel wide at the least, so a zoomed-out map shows structure.
- `basemap-labels`: labels are `text.secondary`, haloed in the land color.
- `basemap-label-font`: labels are set in Roboto, upright, from Kilowatt's own glyph files.
- `map-card`: the map fills the `figure` below the card's header, with no padding.
- `map-controls`: zoom in, zoom out and show all sites are `IconButton`s on an outlined `Paper`.
- `map-scroll-zoom`: cooperative gestures are on, so zoom takes Ctrl or ⌘ with the wheel.
- `map-attribution`: CARTO's and OpenStreetMap's credit is kept, as MapLibre's compact button on the card surface.
- `map-data-colors`: sites take their colors from `mapColors`.
- `map-tooltips`: every site has a tooltip on the menu surface with its name and full value.
- `map-text-alternative`: the `figure` is named by `summary`, and the sites are listed for screen readers.

## Accessibility

- The map draws on a canvas, so the `figure` around it is named by `summary` through `aria-label`, and every site, with its value, is in a list that screen readers read and the screen doesn't show.
- The zoom and fit controls are buttons with names, and reachable by keyboard. MapLibre also zooms and pans with the keyboard once the map has focus.
- A site's dot is 16px, or 12px when `neutral` or the smallest `sized` dot, and the pointer picks it from 6px further out, so every hit area is 24px or more.
- Sites are told apart by name in the tooltip and the list, not by color alone; a highlighted site is named in the summary, and a status map's `detail` line says in words what each color, size or ring means.
- MapLibre jumps camera moves (zoom, fit) to their end when the user asks for reduced motion.
