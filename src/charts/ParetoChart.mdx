# Pareto chart

A Pareto chart shows which few items make up most of a total: annual savings per opportunity, sorted, with the cumulative share. `ParetoChart` is Kilowatt's reference implementation: amCharts 5 (`@amcharts/amcharts5/xy`) run through `useChart`, so it takes the chart theme for the active color scheme (Rules → Charts), inside MUI's `Card` (Material UI v9). It sorts the items largest first and plots their amounts as columns on the left axis, and their cumulative share, 0 to 100%, as a `chart.neutral` line on the right, with a dashed reference line at the threshold (80%). Enertiv's app rebuilds its charts from this one rather than importing it.

## When to use

- Use a Pareto chart to show which few items make up most of a total: savings by opportunity, cost by property, alerts by cause.
- For a ranking where the share of the total doesn't matter, use a horizontal column chart.
- For what moved a total from one value to another, use a waterfall chart.
- For more than about fifteen items, group the smallest into one, or use a table.

## Usage

```tsx
import Card from '@mui/material/Card';
import CardHeader from '@mui/material/CardHeader';
import { ParetoChart } from 'src/charts/ParetoChart';

<Card variant="outlined">
  <CardHeader title="Annual Savings by Opportunity" />
  <ParetoChart
    data={opportunities}
    categoryField="opportunity"
    valueField="savings"
    name="Annual savings"
    unit="USD"
    summary="4 of 12 opportunities make up 80% of the annual savings on offer."
  />
</Card>;
```

Props, with their defaults:

- `data`: the items in any order; the chart sorts them. Keep it stable (a constant or `useMemo`).
- `categoryField`: the field that holds each item's name.
- `valueField`: the field that holds each item's amount.
- `name`: what the columns are, in the legend and tooltips.
- `unit`: above the left axis and in the tooltips.
- `summary`: one sentence, the figure's accessible name.
- `cumulativeName`: what the line is. Default `'Cumulative share'`.
- `threshold`: the share marked by a reference line, in percent, or `null`. Default `80`.
- `height`: the plot's height in pixels. Default `260`.
- `inset`: the padding at the sides and bottom, `2` (16px), `1` (8px) or `0`. Default `2`.

## Variants

- **Savings by opportunity.** Annual savings per opportunity, largest first, with the cumulative share and the 80% threshold: the items left of where the line crosses it are the few that matter most.
- **Without a threshold.** `threshold={null}` drops the reference line, for a total with no cut-off worth marking.

## Do and don't

- Do put the chart in an outlined `Card` (`variant="outlined"`) under a title-only `CardHeader` that says what's measured, straight in the `Card` with no `CardContent`: the chart pads itself through `inset`. The page sets the period.
- Don't put the unit in the card header or a subtitle; it sits with the numbers, on the axis and in the tooltips.
- Do keep the item names short, since each truncates to its column's width.
- Don't pass long descriptions as names; the tooltip names each item in full, and a table can say more.
- Do keep to one unit for the columns.
- Don't mix amounts in different units; the cumulative share only means something for one total.

## MUI departures

- `chart-palette`: the columns take the first `chart.series` color, and the cumulative line takes `chart.neutral`, since it's context.

Theme-wide departures (the surface levels the card sits on, the theme radius) apply too; see Foundations → MUI departures.

## amCharts departures

How this chart differs from stock amCharts 5, from `src/charts/departures.ts`; Foundations → amCharts departures has the reasons.

- `chart-colors`: the chart's chrome takes the palette's roles, and its marks take `chart.series`, `chart.neutral` or the intents as this page says.
- `chart-type`: labels are in the theme's font at the `caption` size, in `text.secondary`.
- `chart-gridlines`: gridlines are full strength in `border.level2`, a step past the divider, so they read on the card.
- `chart-tooltips`: tooltips sit on the menu surface with `text.primary`, outlined and rounded like a menu.
- `value-axis-labels`: axis labels are left-aligned on the left and right-aligned on the right, 8px from the plot, so a left axis lines up with the card's title.
- `legend-items`: legend items have no left padding, so the legend lines up with the card's title.
- `legend-markers`: each legend item is marked by an 8px dot in its series' color.
- `compact-axis-numbers`: axis numbers are compact in the viewer's locale (1.5K, 60K).
- `number-separators`: a tooltip's exact value takes the viewer's thousands and decimal separators, as the axis numbers do.
- `reduced-motion`: the chart animates only when the user hasn't asked for reduced motion.
- `chart-card`: the chart sits straight in an outlined `Card` under a title-only `CardHeader` and pads itself 16px (`inset`).
- `value-axis-side`: two units take the left and the right: the amounts' axis on the left, the share's on the right.
- `axis-units`: each value axis has its unit above it, the amounts' and `%` (`axisUnit`).
- `full-value-tooltips`: every column has a tooltip with its item, exact amount and unit, and every point of the line its cumulative share (`tooltipNumberFormat`).
- `chart-text-alternative`: the `figure` around the chart is named by `summary` (`aria-label`).
- `legend-layout`: the legend sits below the plot and names the columns and the line, each with its unit.
- `reference-lines`: the threshold is a dashed `text.secondary` line on the share axis, labeled at the plot's left edge.
- `point-markers`: every item on the cumulative line is marked with a hollow dot, since each is one item's share.
- `pareto-cumulative`: the items are sorted largest first, and the cumulative share is a `chart.neutral` line on a 0–100% right axis.

## Accessibility

- The legend names the columns and the line with their units, and the threshold is labeled with its value.
- Item names truncate to their column's width; every column shows its item's full name and exact amount, and every point of the line its cumulative share, in a tooltip on hover.
- The chart draws on a canvas, so the `figure` around it is named by `summary` through `aria-label`. Say how many items make up the threshold.
- For exact values, offer the data as a table beside the chart.
