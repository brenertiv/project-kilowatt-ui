# MonthLinesChart

`MonthLinesChart` draws a line per year across the twelve months, for a year-over-year chart: each year's January over every other January. `LineChart` can't draw it, since its months sit on one date axis. It's built as the reference charts are (Rules → Charts): amCharts 5 through `useChart`, the lines in `chart.series` order through `seriesColors`, the unit above the value axis on the right, a legend past one line, the full value in every tooltip, a dot on every month so a lone month with data still shows, and a gap where a month has no data, never a line across it. It sits in a `figure` named by `summary`, in an outlined MUI `Card` (`@mui/material`, Material UI v9) under a `CardHeader`.

<Canvas of={MonthLinesChartStories.YearOverYear} />

## When to use

- Use it to compare years month by month: a property's electricity, water or gas this year against the years before.
- For a value over continuous time, use `LineChart`.
- For two totals, this period against the same months a year earlier, use `ComparisonBars`.
- For monthly totals of one year, use `ColumnChart`.

## Usage

```tsx
import Card from '@mui/material/Card';
import CardHeader from '@mui/material/CardHeader';
import { MonthLinesChart } from 'src/charts/MonthLinesChart';
import { TruncatedText } from 'src/components/TruncatedText';

const data = [{ month: 'Jan', y2024: 59100, y2025: 57400 } /* …to Dec */];
const series = [
  { field: 'y2024', name: '2024' },
  { field: 'y2025', name: '2025' },
];

<Card variant="outlined">
  <CardHeader title={<TruncatedText>Electricity by Month</TruncatedText>} />
  <MonthLinesChart data={data} categoryField="month" series={series} unit="kWh" summary="Electricity by month, 2024 and 2025: …" />
</Card>
```

Pass twelve rows, January first, with a field per year; leave a field out of a month that has no data. List the years oldest first. Keep `data` and `series` stable (`useMemo`).

## Variants

- **Several years**: the lines take the series colors in order, with a legend under the plot.
- **One year**: one line in the first series color, no legend.

## Do and don't

- Do list the years oldest first, so the newest takes the latest series slot and the legend reads in time order.
- Don't give the current year a different line style; variants differ by style, years are different series.
- Do leave a month out where there's no data.
- Don't fill a missing month with 0; the line breaks there instead.
- Do keep to six years at most.
- Don't add a seventh color; past six years, show a table.

## MUI departures

- `chart-palette`: lines take `chart.series` in order or by `slot`, and neutral lines take `chart.neutral`.

Theme-wide departures (the surface levels the card sits on, the theme radius) apply too; see Foundations → MUI departures.

## amCharts departures

How this chart differs from stock amCharts 5, from `src/charts/departures.ts`; Foundations → amCharts departures has the reasons.

- `chart-colors`: the chart's chrome takes the palette's roles, and series take `chart.series` in order, used as they are.
- `chart-type`: labels are in the theme's font at the `caption` size, in `text.secondary`.
- `chart-gridlines`: gridlines are full strength in `border.level2`, a step past the divider, so they read on the card.
- `chart-tooltips`: tooltips sit on the menu surface with `text.primary`, outlined and rounded like a menu.
- `value-axis-labels`: value-axis numbers are left-aligned, 8px from the plot.
- `legend-items`: legend items have no left padding, so the legend lines up with the axis and the title.
- `legend-markers`: each legend item is marked by an 8px dot in its series' color, as the donut's legend is.
- `compact-axis-numbers`: axis numbers are compact in the viewer's locale (1.5K, 60K).
- `reduced-motion`: the chart animates only when the user hasn't asked for reduced motion.
- `chart-card`: the chart sits straight in an outlined `Card` under a title-only `CardHeader` and pads itself 16px (`inset`).
- `value-axis-side`: the one value axis sits on the right.
- `axis-units`: the value axis has its unit above it, left-aligned with its numbers (`axisUnit`).
- `full-value-tooltips`: every point has a tooltip with the year, the month, the exact value and the unit (`tooltipNumberFormat`).
- `number-separators`: a tooltip's exact value takes the viewer's thousands and decimal separators, as the axis numbers do.
- `chart-text-alternative`: the `figure` around the chart is named by `summary` (`aria-label`).
- `legend-layout`: the legend sits below the plot in up to three columns, for two or more years.
- `point-markers`: every month is marked with a 4px dot ringed in the card surface, so a month with data between two without still shows.

## Accessibility

- The chart draws on a canvas; the `figure` around it is named by `summary`, one sentence with the numbers that matter and the months without data.
- Every point's tooltip names its year and month and gives the full value with its unit.
- The years are told apart by the legend's names as well as by color.

## Related

- `LineChart` for values over continuous time.
- `ComparisonBars` for two totals side by side.
- `ColumnChart` for one year's monthly totals.
