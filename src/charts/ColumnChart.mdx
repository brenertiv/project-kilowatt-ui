# Column chart

A column chart compares values across categories: buildings, months, utilities. `ColumnChart` is Kilowatt's reference implementation: amCharts 5 (`@amcharts/amcharts5`) run through `useChart`, so it takes the chart theme for the active color scheme (Rules → Charts). Enertiv's app rebuilds its charts from this one rather than importing it.

<Canvas of={ColumnChartStories.Categories} />

## When to use

- Use a column chart to compare a measure across categories, or across periods that read as buckets (months, years).
- For a continuous measure over time (daily, hourly), use a line chart.
- For parts of one whole at a single point, with six parts or fewer, use a donut chart; for parts of a total across several categories, stack the columns.
- For many categories with long names, or values people look up rather than compare, use a table.

## Usage

```tsx
import { ColumnChart } from 'src/charts/ColumnChart';

const series = [
  { field: 'supply', name: 'Supply', slot: 0 },
  { field: 'delivery', name: 'Delivery', slot: 0, striped: true },
];

<Card variant="outlined">
  <CardHeader title="Electricity Cost" />
  <ColumnChart data={bills} categoryField="month" series={series} stacked unit="USD" summary="Cost peaked in July; delivery was 31% of the year's electricity cost." />
</Card>
```

Each row of `data` is one category: its label in `categoryField` and a number per series. Keep `data` and `series` stable (constants or `useMemo`), since the chart is rebuilt when they change.

Props beyond those, with their defaults:

- `categoryLabelField`: the field that holds the label to show on the axis and in the tooltip, where `categoryField` holds a key instead. Default: `categoryField`.
- `integerValues`: whole-number ticks on the value axis, for a count of records. Default `false`.
- `onCategoryClick`: called with a category's row when one of its columns is clicked. Default none.
- `onMissingClick`: called with a category's row when its place in the plot is clicked and no series has a value in it. Default none.

## Variants

- **Categories.** One measure across categories. Every column takes one color: the categories are named on the axis, so coloring them apart adds nothing.
- **Side by side.** Up to six series per category, told apart by `chart.series` color.
- **Stacked.** `stacked` puts the series in one column per category, for parts that add up to a total. The chart orders each stack by color, dark to light across the whole stack: darkest at the bottom of a column and lightest at the top, lightest at the left of a `horizontal` bar and darkest at the right, both reversed in dark. Every series keeps its color, the neutral Other takes its place by its lightness like the rest, and the legend keeps the order of `series`.
- **Ranking.** `horizontal` lays the bars out across, categories down the left in `data` order and values along the bottom, so long names fit. Sort `data` largest first, and keep one color. A name longer than 160px truncates, or than two fifths of the chart's width where the chart is narrow (a phone), so the bars keep the rest; the tooltip gives it in full.
- **Grouped into Other.** Past six colored series, the five with the largest totals keep their colors and the rest are summed into one neutral series named `otherLabel`.
- **Striped.** `striped` marks a variant of another series (delivery beside supply, estimated beside actual). Give both the same `slot`: they keep one hue, and the stripes tell them apart.
- **Category click.** `onCategoryClick` makes the chart narrow what's beside it (a table of the records behind a ranking): a click on a column calls it with that category's row, as `data` gave it (a grouped chart's row also carries the summed `other` field), and the columns take a hand pointer. The page does the narrowing. Keep the handler stable (`useCallback`), since the chart is rebuilt when it changes.
- **Whole numbers.** `integerValues` keeps the value axis to whole numbers, for a count of records (alerts, work orders), where amCharts would otherwise tick at 0.5.
- **Category labels.** A category axis keys its rows by `categoryField`, so two rows with one label would share a bar. Where labels can repeat (two buildings with one name), put a unique key in `categoryField` and the name in `categoryLabelField`: the axis and the tooltip show the name, and each row keeps its own bar.
- **Missing period.** A category with no value draws no column, never a zero. With `onMissingClick`, a click on its place in the plot calls the handler with its row (the row is in `data` with its label and no series field), and the pointer is a hand there, so a page can open what explains the gap. Keep the handler stable, like `onCategoryClick`.
- **Still being billed.** On a chart by month, `stillBilling` names the trailing months with bills for fewer than nine in ten of the properties in scope (`isStillBilling` from `src/charts/stillBilling` holds the rule), each with its count: `{ month: 'Oct', billed: 11, properties: 18 }`. Every column of such a month is a dashed outline in its series' hue with no fill, its tooltip adds "Still being billed: 11 of 18 properties", and the same line sits under the chart. Leave a month out once it passes nine in ten, and it draws solid.

## Do and don't

- Do put the chart in an outlined `Card` (`variant="outlined"`) under a title-only `CardHeader` that says what's measured, straight in the `Card` with no `CardContent`: the chart pads itself through `inset`, 16px by default to line up with the title and any `CardContent`, `1` (8px) for a tighter fit, `0` to run edge to edge. The page sets the period.
- Don't put the unit in the card header or a subtitle; `unit` shows it above the value axis and in the tooltip, next to the numbers.
- Do keep one color when the categories are on the axis.
- Don't color each column differently to make the chart livelier; color then suggests a meaning that isn't there.
- Do stripe a variant in its series' hue, with the same `slot`.
- Don't give supply and delivery, or actual and estimated, two different hues; they're one meaning.
- Do mark a trailing month with `stillBilling` while fewer than nine in ten of the properties in scope have a bill for it.
- Don't draw a month still being billed in a lighter tint, with a ≈ sign or as a solid column; a partial month then reads as a drop.
- Do stack only parts that add up to a meaningful total.
- Don't order `series` to put a part at the bottom of a stack; the chart orders a stack by color, and its order can differ between light and dark.
- Don't stack values in different units, or values that overlap.
- Do give `onCategoryClick` and `onMissingClick` a second way through that isn't the pointer: the same rows as a table, or a control beside the chart.
- Don't make a column or a gap the only way to reach something; the chart draws on a canvas, and its columns take no keyboard focus.
- Do use `integerValues` only for counts.
- Don't round a measured value's axis with it; a measure has fractions.
- Do leave a category with no value in `data` with its label and no number.
- Don't send a missing value as 0; a zero draws as a reading of nothing.
- Do write `summary` as one sentence with the numbers that matter.
- Do use `horizontal` bars for a ranking or for long category names, sorted largest first.
- Don't color each bar of a ranking or draw each one as its own series; one series in one color reads as one measure.
- Don't pass more than six colored series expecting six colors; the chart groups the rest into Other, so name `otherLabel` for what they are, or use a table.

## MUI departures

- `chart-palette`: series take `chart.series` in order or by `slot`, and neutral series take `chart.neutral`.

Theme-wide departures (the surface levels the card sits on, the theme radius) apply too; see Foundations → MUI departures.

## amCharts departures

How this chart differs from stock amCharts 5, from `src/charts/departures.ts`; Foundations → amCharts departures has the reasons.

- `chart-colors`: the chart's chrome takes the palette's roles, and series take `chart.series` in order, used as they are.
- `chart-type`: labels are in the theme's font at the `caption` size, in `text.secondary`.
- `chart-gridlines`: gridlines are full strength in `border.level2`, a step past the divider, so they read on the card.
- `chart-tooltips`: tooltips sit on the menu surface with `text.primary`, outlined and rounded like a menu.
- `value-axis-labels`: value-axis numbers are left-aligned, 8px from the plot, so a left axis lines up with the card's title.
- `legend-items`: legend items have no left padding, so the legend lines up with the axis and the title.
- `legend-markers`: each legend item is marked by an 8px dot in its series' color, as the donut's legend is.
- `compact-axis-numbers`: axis numbers are compact in the viewer's locale (1.5K, 60K).
- `reduced-motion`: the chart animates only when the user hasn't asked for reduced motion.
- `chart-card`: the chart sits straight in an outlined `Card` under a title-only `CardHeader` and pads itself 16px (`inset`).
- `value-axis-side`: one value axis sits on the right; two units take the left and the right.
- `axis-units`: each value axis has its unit above it, left-aligned with its numbers (`axisUnit`).
- `stack-order`: a stack runs dark to light across all its colors, ordered by lightness (`stackOrder`), reversed in dark.
- `full-value-tooltips`: every hoverable item has a tooltip with its exact value and unit (`tooltipNumberFormat`).
- `number-separators`: a tooltip's exact value takes the viewer's thousands and decimal separators, as the axis numbers do.
- `chart-text-alternative`: the `figure` around the chart is named by `summary` (`aria-label`).
- `legend-layout`: the legend sits below the plot in up to three columns, for two or more series.
- `still-being-billed`: `stillBilling` draws a trailing month's columns as dashed outlines in their own hue, with the count of properties billed in the tooltip and under the chart.

## Accessibility

- Every point, column or slice shows its exact value and unit in a tooltip on hover, since axis numbers are rounded to compact form (1.5K).
- The chart draws on a canvas, so the `figure` around it is named by `summary` through `aria-label`. The summary isn't shown on screen; it's the chart's text alternative.
- amCharts adds keyboard focus and screen-reader announcements for the legend and columns.
- `onCategoryClick` and `onMissingClick` answer the pointer only: a column and a gap aren't reachable by keyboard. Offer the same rows as a table or as controls beside the chart, and name a missing period in `summary`.
- Series are told apart by name in the legend and tooltip as well as color, and a striped variant by pattern too.
- A month still being billed is told by its dashed outline, by its tooltip and by the line under the chart, a `figcaption` screen readers reach. Say it in `summary` too, since the summary is what names the chart.
- For detailed values, offer the data as a table beside the chart.
