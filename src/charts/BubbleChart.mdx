# Bubble chart

A bubble chart places each item by two measures and sizes it by a third: each opportunity's upfront cost across, its annual savings up, and the emissions it avoids as its area. `BubbleChart` is Kilowatt's reference implementation: amCharts 5 (`@amcharts/amcharts5/xy`) run through `useChart`, so it takes the chart theme for the active color scheme (Rules → Charts), inside MUI's `Card` (Material UI v9). A bubble's area, not its radius, is its amount; bubbles take the first series color or a color per group, ringed in the card surface and drawn largest first. Both axes start at 0, and a caption under the plot names the three measures. Enertiv's app rebuilds its charts from this one rather than importing it.

## When to use

- Use a bubble chart to compare items on three measures at once, where where they sit matters: opportunities by cost, savings and emissions avoided.
- For two measures, use the same chart with the size even, or a table.
- For one measure across items, use a column chart.
- For more than about thirty items, or bubbles that hide each other, use a table with sorting.

## Usage

```tsx
import Card from '@mui/material/Card';
import CardHeader from '@mui/material/CardHeader';
import { BubbleChart } from 'src/charts/BubbleChart';

<Card variant="outlined">
  <CardHeader title="Opportunities by Cost and Savings" />
  <BubbleChart
    data={opportunities}
    labelField="opportunity"
    x={{ field: 'cost', name: 'Upfront cost', unit: 'USD' }}
    y={{ field: 'savings', name: 'Annual savings', unit: 'USD' }}
    size={{ field: 'avoided', name: 'Avoided emissions', unit: 'tCO₂e/yr' }}
    summary="The cheapest opportunities save the most for what they cost."
  />
</Card>;
```

Props, with their defaults:

- `data`: the items, one bubble each. Keep it stable (a constant or `useMemo`).
- `labelField`: the field that holds each item's name, in its tooltip.
- `x`: the measure across, `{ field, name, unit }` (`BubbleMeasure`).
- `y`: the measure up.
- `size`: the measure each bubble's area shows.
- `summary`: one sentence, the figure's accessible name.
- `groupField`: the field that holds each item's group. Default: none.
- `groups`: `{ value, name? }` in legend order, each with an optional `slot` (`SeriesColor`). Up to six.
- `height`: the plot's height in pixels. Default `320`.
- `inset`: the padding at the sides and bottom, `2` (16px), `1` (8px) or `0`. Default `2`.

## Variants

- **Cost against savings.** Every opportunity in the first series color: where it sits and how big it is are the point.
- **Grouped by kind.** `groupField` and `groups` color the bubbles by the kind of measure, in `chart.series` order, named in a legend under the plot.

## Do and don't

- Do put the chart in an outlined `Card` (`variant="outlined"`) under a title-only `CardHeader` that says what's measured, straight in the `Card` with no `CardContent`: the chart pads itself through `inset`. The page sets the period.
- Don't put the unit in the card header or a subtitle; it sits with the numbers, on the axis and in the tooltips.
- Do name all three measures; the caption under the plot does it from `x`, `y` and `size`.
- Don't rely on the axes to say what they measure; they carry only their units.
- Do group by color only when the groups matter to the question.
- Don't color every bubble differently; give each item a name in its tooltip instead.

## MUI departures

- `chart-palette`: bubbles take the first `chart.series` color, or `chart.series` in order or by `slot` per group, ringed in the card surface.

Theme-wide departures (the surface levels the card sits on, the theme radius) apply too; see Foundations → MUI departures.

## amCharts departures

How this chart differs from stock amCharts 5, from `src/charts/departures.ts`; Foundations → amCharts departures has the reasons.

- `chart-colors`: the chart's chrome takes the palette's roles, and its marks take `chart.series`, `chart.neutral` or the intents as this page says.
- `chart-type`: labels are in the theme's font at the `caption` size, in `text.secondary`.
- `chart-gridlines`: gridlines are full strength in `border.level2`, a step past the divider, so they read on the card.
- `chart-tooltips`: tooltips sit on the menu surface with `text.primary`, outlined and rounded like a menu.
- `value-axis-labels`: value-axis numbers are right-aligned against the plot, 8px from it.
- `compact-axis-numbers`: axis numbers are compact in the viewer's locale (1.5K, 60K).
- `number-separators`: a tooltip's exact value takes the viewer's thousands and decimal separators, as the axis numbers do.
- `reduced-motion`: the chart animates only when the user hasn't asked for reduced motion.
- `chart-card`: the chart sits straight in an outlined `Card` under a title-only `CardHeader` and pads itself 16px (`inset`).
- `value-axis-side`: the vertical value axis sits on the left; the horizontal one along the bottom has its unit at its end.
- `axis-units`: the vertical value axis has its unit above it (`axisUnit`); the caption names both measures.
- `full-value-tooltips`: every bubble has a tooltip with its name and all three measures, exact and with their units (`tooltipNumberFormat`).
- `chart-text-alternative`: the `figure` around the chart is named by `summary` (`aria-label`).
- `bubble-area`: a bubble's radius is the square root of its share of the largest, so its area is its amount, from 5px to 28px, ringed in the card surface and drawn largest first; groups are named in an HTML legend under the plot.

## Accessibility

- The caption under the plot names the three measures and their units in text.
- Every bubble shows its name and all three measures in a tooltip on hover.
- The chart draws on a canvas, so the `figure` around it is named by `summary` through `aria-label`. Say which items stand out and why.
- For exact values, offer the data as a table beside the chart.
